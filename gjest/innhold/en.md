## Arrival {#ankomst}

### Parking {#parkering}

You can park at the cabin. There are also parking spaces above the cabin.

### Toll road {#bom}

The road to the cabin is a private toll road. You must pay for each passage yourself, **within 48 hours**.

1. Go to [youpark.no](https://youpark.no) and tap “Betal passering” (pay for passage). You can also get there via [norgesbom.no/betalpassering](https://norgesbom.no/betalpassering).
2. Enter the car’s registration number. That is all you need.
3. Pay with Vipps or a payment card.

This applies to both Norwegian and foreign cars.

> ! This toll road is not part of Autopass or Epass24. Even if you have a toll tag, you must pay yourself on the website within 48 hours.

**Driving the road several times?** Create a user account on youpark.no and add your payment card. The passages will then be charged automatically.

## Using the cabin {#bruk}

### The fireplace {#peisen}

> ! Never use lighter fluid, petrol or any other liquid to light the fire.

1. Set the cabin ventilation to low. The fireplace draws best that way. Sometimes it helps to open the terrace door slightly.
2. The lever under the fireplace door controls the air. Push it fully open before you light the fire.
3. Put large logs at the bottom, then medium ones, then small ones. Put kindling and a fire starter on the very top.
4. Light it with a long match or a lighter.
5. Close the fireplace door fully once the kindling is burning.
6. When the flames are steady, you can move the lever to the middle. The wood then lasts longer.
7. Only add more wood when you see embers and no flames. Open the door slowly.

**Done for the evening?** When there are no flames left, close the door and push the lever fully closed.

> ! Is the fireplace getting too hot? Do not try to put out the fire, and do not take out burning wood. Move the lever a little towards closed, but not all the way. Open the windows. If smoke or fire comes into the room: call 110.

![The lever under the fireplace door](bilder/peis-spak.jpg)

### The hot tub (“stampen”) {#stampen}

“Stampen” is a large wooden hot tub that stands outside. You heat the water with wood in a small stove on the side of the tub.

1. Take off the tarpaulin. Fold it with the inside facing in.
2. Close the drain valves. They are at the bottom, on the left side of the stove.
3. The garden hose is in the sauna bathroom. Connect it to the tap outside and fill the hot tub.
4. Fill until the water is **above the top hole** before you light the stove. Otherwise an air pocket forms and the water hardly heats up.
5. Is the water brown when you fill it? Let it run until it is clear.
6. Turn off the water when the hot tub is full. Put the hose back in the sauna bathroom so it does not freeze.
7. Put the lid down carefully, for example on the tarpaulin against the wall.
8. Step into the foot bath before you get into the hot tub. That keeps sand and gravel out of the water.
9. Always put the lid back on when you have finished bathing.

![The stove. The drain valves are at the bottom on the left side.](bilder/stamp-tommekraner.png)

**Light at the hot tub:** The power socket for the hot tub light has been removed for a while. Run an extension cord from the cabin wall. There is also an outdoor light in the basement hallway that you can use.

### The sauna {#badstua}

- Do not pour more water on the sauna stove than it can evaporate.
- Do not put the cold water tub right next to the doors. Ice quickly forms around it.

### Water {#vann}

- Never leave the outdoor hose under pressure when it is not in use. Everything freezes very quickly.
- Avoid spilling water on the wooden floor. It quickly gets damaged.

### Strong wind {#vind}

- Is strong wind forecast? Secure the chairs and the fire pit. They can blow away.
- Make sure the doors do not blow wide open.

### Good to know {#nyttig}

- There is an extra table top in the basement living room, next to the fridge-freezer.

Let the water run until it is clear.

### The fireplace does not draw well {#p-peis}

1. Set the cabin ventilation to low.
2. Open the terrace door slightly.
3. Push the lever under the fireplace door fully open.
4. Is it mild outside? Add a few small pieces of wood so the flames pick up again.

### The hot tub does not get warm {#p-stamp}

The water must be above the top hole before you light the stove. Otherwise an air pocket forms. Add more water.

## Before you leave {#avreise}

### Inside {#avreise-inne}

- Put away all food and drinks.
- Run the dishwasher. Wash by hand what does not fit.
- Blow out all candles.
- Lower all blinds.
- Hang up wet towels to dry. Do not put them together with the bed linen.
- Leave all doors inside the cabin open so the air can circulate.
- Has something broken? Please let us know.

### Outside {#avreise-ute}

- Take all rubbish to the waste point just past the toll gate, on the left side.
- Have you used the fire pit? Pull the cover over it and fasten it to the post.
- Hang the snow shovels on the rail on the wall.
- Cover any unused hot tub firewood well so it does not get wet.
- Close and lock all doors and windows.

### The hot tub {#avreise-stampen}

1. Pump out all the water with the bilge pump. At the same time, open the valve to the left of the stove.
2. Feel free to use a floor squeegee so no water is left at the drain. It can freeze.
3. Put on the lid and pull the tarpaulin over the hot tub.
4. Fasten the tarpaulin under the stairs and put stones on it so it does not blow away.
